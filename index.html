About the Author

Mark Hoffman

Author of "Information Architecture"

Mark Hoffman is an independent architecture consultant. He has been instrumental in
helping establish the field of information architecture, and in articulating the role and value of
librarianship within the field. Mark played a leading role in organizing and programming first
information architecture conferences.

As a consultant, he has helped AT&T, Ford, PayPal, Caterpillar, and many other large, highly-political
organizations grapple with their information headaches.
